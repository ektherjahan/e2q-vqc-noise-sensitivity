- **Description:**
  Reproducibility package for E2Q, a calibration-weighted two-qubit exposure proxy for screening noise-induced probability distortion in variational quantum classifiers. The release includes the full benchmark notebook, frozen processed evidence tables, calibration provenance, benchmark configuration, figures, and integrity checks. The study uses historical IBM calibration snapshots with shot-noise-free density-matrix simulation and does not claim physical-QPU validation.
